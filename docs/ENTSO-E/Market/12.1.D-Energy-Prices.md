# 12.1.D Energy Prices

> Part of the [Market](./README.md) collection · [API overview](../ENTSOe-docs.md)

Request Limits:

- One year range limit applies
    

Response Limits:

- 100 documents (TimeSeries) - Offset parameter can be used in case the request matches more than 100 publications.
    
- Minimum time interval in query response ranges from part of day to 29 hours, depending on selected Contract_MarketAgreement.Type
### Method: GET
>```
>{{baseUrl}}?documentType=A44&periodStart=202407272200&periodEnd=202407282200&out_Domain=10YAT-APG------L&in_Domain=10YAT-APG------L
>```
### Query Params

|Param|value|
|---|---|
|documentType|A44|
|periodStart|202407272200|
|periodEnd|202407282200|
|out_Domain|10YAT-APG------L|
|in_Domain|10YAT-APG------L|
|contract_MarketAgreement.type|A01|
|classificationSequence_AttributeInstanceComponent.position|1|
|offset|0|
